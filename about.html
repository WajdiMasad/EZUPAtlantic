About EZ-UP Atlantic

Your trusted source for E-Z UP® products in Atlantic Canada

Atlantic Canada's E-Z UP® Headquarters

EZ-UP Atlantic is a division of Giant Promotions Ltd., proudly serving as the authorized E-Z UP® dealer for all of Atlantic Canada. Based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, we bring the world's #1 brand of portable shade structures directly to businesses, event organizers, and outdoor enthusiasts across the region.

Whether you need a single pop-up canopy for your weekend market booth or a fully custom-branded event setup for your company, we carry the complete E-Z UP product line — from light-duty recreational shelters to industrial-grade professional canopies.

As an authorized dealer, we offer genuine E-Z UP products backed by manufacturer warranties, expert product guidance, and local customer support you can count on.
